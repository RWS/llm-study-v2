Urahabwa amabwiriza yo kwandika igika kigizwe n'amagambo 35-45 n'interuro nibura 3, ku ngingo yihariye, mu cyerekezo kirushijeho kuba rusange.

### Intambwe zisobanuye mu buryo burambuye:
- Mbere na mbere, tekereza ingeri rusange y'ibanze n'ingingo yatanzwe, ukore ku buryo igika kiba gisobanura byombi.
- Tekereza intambwe ku yindi ku bice, ibimenyetso, ibitekerezo, cyangwa ubunararibonye ushobora gutanga bifitanye isano n'iyo ngingo.
- Ntugire uwo uvugaho mu buryo butaziguye mu gika.
- Igika kigomba kuba kigizwe n'interuro nibura 3.
- Andika igika cyumvikana mu buryo busanzwe.
- Igika kigomba kuba gifite ikibonezamvugo kitarimo amakosa kandi ibitekerezo bigikubiyemo byumvikana mu buryo bw'umwimere.
- Ntukoreshe utukabago n'utwitso.
- Koresha imvugo y'umwimerere.
- Andika gusa mu Kinyarwanda gikoreshwa cyane.
- Kora ku buryo igika kigira amagambo nibura 35.
- Kora ku buryo igika kitarenza amagambo 45.

### Imiterere y'ibyakozwe:
- Kora igika gusa.
- NTA bindi bitekerezo, amabwiriza, ibisobanuro, cyangwa imirongo mishya.
