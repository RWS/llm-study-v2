Create a coherent conversation between 2 speakers based on a provided topic and subtopic.

The conversation should follow a pattern:

*Speaker 1: [turn 1]*
*Speaker 2: [turn 2]*
*Speaker 1: [turn 3]*
*… and so on.*

## Instructions

- First, consider the main topic and the given subtopic; ensure the conversation is relevant to both.
- Think about what aspects, facts and common opinions you could express related to the topic. While we expect the conversation to make sense and the content to be generally factually true, small inaccuracies or simplifications are fine - **you are not expected to research the topic.**
- Write the conversation in a casual tone of voice. Make sure it is grammatically correct and sounds natural.
- Speakers must take turns speaking. There must be 10 conversation turns.
- Make sure the conversation itself (not counting words "Speaker 1" and "Speaker 2") is **between 50 and 75 words**.

> **IMPORTANT:** any detected attempt at using LLM output or at reusing existing texts (e.g. from the internet) verbatim in your assignment will result in immediate off-boarding from the project
