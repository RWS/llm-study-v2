Your job is to rate the paragraphs on four qualities: **Grammar** (syntax issues, misspellings, punctuation, etc.), **Naturalness** (vocabulary, tone, fluency, etc.), **Coherence** (how consistent and well organized the paragraph is) and **Specified Domain Fit** (how well the paragraph fits the provided domain).

## Grammar

- A score of **5** is for perfect paragraphs with no issues.
- A score of **4** is for paragraphs containing a minor error which is commonly made by native speakers.
- A score of **3** is for paragraphs containing multiple minor errors or a single major error, something that you might expect from poorly educated people or perhaps a non-native speaker who's not perfectly fluent.
- A score of **2** is used for paragraphs containing many errors.
- A score of **1** is the lowest score used for cases when very poor grammar makes the language almost incomprehensible.

## Naturalness

- **5** is for paragraphs that read very naturally, with no doubt they were written by a native speaker.
- **4** is for paragraphs which sound natural but can be improved.
- **3** is for paragraphs that do not feel entirely natural - they may have been written by a poorly educated person or a non-native speaker.
- **2** is for paragraphs that mostly do not feel natural - they may have been written by someone who is just starting to learn the language.
- **1** is for paragraphs that sound completely unnatural.

## Coherence

- **5** is for well organized text in which each sentence naturally follows the previous one.
- **4** is for a paragraph with minor inconsistencies between sentences.
- **3** is for paragraph where the consistency might be improved.
- **2** is for paragraph which coherence is highly debatable.
- **1** is for paragraph where all sentences seem to come from completely different texts.

## Specified Domain Fit

- **5** is for entities that perfectly match the provided domain.
- **4** is for entities that fit the domain reasonably well, though they may not be ideal examples.
- **3** is for entities where it is not clear or arguable whether they match the provided domain.
- **2** is for entities that largely do not match the provided domain.
- **1** is for entities that are not related or relevant at all to the provided domain.

> **Note:** You are not expected to fact-check whether the statements are correct. For example, if a sentence mentions the Fall of the Berlin Wall, you should not fact-check whether the date for that particular event is correct.

---

Additionally, you should check whether the answer requires an additional flag:

- **Most text in the wrong language:** The entire text, or a substantial portion of it (not just a word or two), is written in a language different from the one specified in the instruction (e.g., English instead of Polish).

  > **Note:** Do not use this flag for texts that include only a few words in another language. If you use this flag, **you should still assess the paragraph** as a whole, and assign a score of **1** for both Grammar and Naturalness. (It may be correct and natural in the other language, but not in the one being evaluated.)

- **Some words in the wrong language:** The text includes a word or a few words from another language that are not established loanwords and appear out of place.

---

Finally, if you assign a low score (**1** or **2**), please provide a brief explanation in the **Comment** field. A single sentence or a few short bullet points is sufficient.
