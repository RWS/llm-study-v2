Your job is to rate the conversations above in three metrics: **Grammar** (syntax issues, misspellings, punctuation, etc.), **Naturalness** (vocabulary, tone, fluency etc.) and **Coherence**.

## Grammar

- A score of **5** is for perfect conversations with no issues.
- A score of **4** is for conversations containing a minor error which is commonly made by native speakers.
- A score of **3** is for conversations containing multiple minor errors or a single major error, something that you might expect from poorly educated people or perhaps a non-native speaker who's not perfectly fluent.
- A score of **2** is used for conversations containing many errors.
- A score of **1** is the lowest score used for cases when very poor grammar makes the language almost incomprehensible.

## Naturalness

- **5** is for conversations that read very naturally, with no doubt they were written by a native speaker.
- **4** is for conversations which sound natural but can be improved.
- **3** is for conversations that do not feel entirely natural - they may have been written by a poorly educated person or a non-native speaker.
- **2** is for conversations that mostly do not feel natural - they may have been written by someone who is just starting to learn the language.
- **1** is for conversations that sound completely unnatural.

## Coherence

- **5** is for well-organized conversation. Each utterance naturally follows from the previous one, with no logical gaps or jumps. The flow is smooth and easy to follow.
- **4** is for a mostly coherent conversation, with only minor inconsistencies or slightly awkward transitions that don't significantly disrupt understanding.
- **3** is for conversation with some noticeable inconsistencies or jumps in logic. The flow is understandable but could be improved.
- **2** is for conversation that has significant coherence issues. Several utterances feel disconnected or hard to relate to the surrounding context.
- **1** is for paragraph where all sentences seem to come from completely different texts.

If the text is a conversation, you should judge the conversation as a whole. Any issues with continuity of the conversation should reduce the Coherence score. The conversation can use quotation marks and should not be penalized for that, unless it is inconsistent within the same conversation. Using "Speaker **1**" or just "**1**" to denominate speakers is acceptable, as long as it is consistent within the same conversation.
